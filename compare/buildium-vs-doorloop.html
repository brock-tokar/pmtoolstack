Verdict

Buildium starts from $62/mo with flat tiers and lists Canada and US availability, which makes it the safer pick for growing managers, especially in Canada. DoorLoop prices per unit with minimums from $199/mo and lists the US. Run your own unit count through both.

Questions

Which is cheaper?

At low unit counts, Buildium Essential from $62/mo is lower than the DoorLoop One minimum of $199/mo. At higher counts, compare the per unit math against Buildium Growth and Premium.

Does DoorLoop work in Canada?

DoorLoop lists the US. Check with the vendor if you manage in Canada.

Is there an annual discount?

Buildium offers 10% off annual billing. Check DoorLoop for current terms.
